Rook Privacy Policy

Rook is a private assistant for its owner's personal use. With the owner's consent, Rook verifies the selected Google account and reads that account's Google Tasks, Calendar events, and free/busy information to show personal tasks, schedules, and availability. This Google connection is read-only.

Rook stores the connection credentials encrypted on the host computer. Briefings may be saved in the owner's local browser chat history. Rook does not sell or share Google user data with third parties. This public website contains no account data and does not connect to Google Tasks or Calendar.

The owner can revoke Rook's access in Google Account settings and clear saved briefings by clearing Rook's browser site data.
